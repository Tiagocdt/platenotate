How this works

Three annotation levels share one idea — every field is a column you define, with a type: categorical, binary, range, or free text. Nothing is hard-coded; the medaka fields are just suggestions you can accept, edit, or ignore.

  • Well (main): tag wells. Rubber-band drag or click to select, then click a value to assign it to every selected well. + column makes a new one; binary columns carry a default.
  • Plate: whole-folder fields — temperature, date, start time, notes. Click Autofill to pull them from the plate metadata.
  • Image / stage: enable it, scrub the trajectory, and stamp a frame with an Iwamatsu stage (or any image-level column). Saved per timepoint.
