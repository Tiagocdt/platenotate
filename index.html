How this works

Three annotation levels share one idea — every field is a column you define, with a type: categorical, binary, range, or free text. Nothing is hard-coded; the suggested fields are just starting points you can accept, edit, or ignore.

  • Well (main): tag wells. Rubber-band drag or click to select, then click a value to assign it to every selected well. + column makes a new one; binary columns carry a default.
  • Plate: whole-folder fields — temperature, date, start time, notes. Click Autofill to pull them from the plate metadata.
  • Image: scrub the trajectory and stamp a frame with a stage or any image-level column. The z / rot faders under the image also set focus / rotation keyframes when their button is switched on. Saved per timepoint.

Open a data folder

Point PlateNotate at a folder that contains your plate folders — on this computer or on a network share. Your annotations are saved there (or wherever you choose in Settings), so you can pick up where you left off.
